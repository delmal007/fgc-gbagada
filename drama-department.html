About Ministry

In the Drama Department, we tell God’s story in a way that would touch hearts and bring the message to life. We are a small but creative group of individuals with a passion for acting and storytelling through drama.

From regular powerful skits to full drama productions on special occasions, we use the stage to share the Gospel in a way that words alone sometimes cannot. Our performances speak of hope, redemption, forgiveness, and God’s love, reaching people of all ages and backgrounds.

Behind each scene is prayer, teamwork, and a desire to glorify God. Rehearsals are more than practice, they are moments of growth, laughter, and fellowship. Each of our members plays a role in bringing the vision to life, whether on stage or behind the scenes.

Today, our department continues to inspire, challenge, and uplift through the art of storytelling. Our mission is clear: to use our gifts to make Jesus known, one scene at a time.
